Show the FLOOR on your UI

Every FLOOR launch has a floor: a price in its quote token that the program's own money stands behind, and that never falls. We ask for two things: the FLOOR line on the chart and the FLOOR price on the token page. Anything more is a bonus. It is a request, not a requirement, and it is highly appreciated. Below is what it looks like, then the code.

The minimum

  1. The FLOOR line on the price chart. #FF6A1A, 2 px, a step line, labelled FLOOR, under the price.
  2. The FLOOR price on the token page, next to the price.

Bonus

  • % backed (floor ÷ price)
  • A FLOOR column in lists and screeners
  • The FLOOR price in a wallet's holding row
  • An alert near the floor

Rules of thumb

  • No line before graduation. Never draw a missing floor as 0.
  • It only steps up, in the quote token.
  • Keep it after graduation: same program, same pair.
  • In market-cap mode, scale it by the same supply as the price.
